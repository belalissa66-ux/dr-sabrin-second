Dr. Sabrina

Dr. Sabrina studied veterinary medicine because she's always been drawn to animals, not just to the science of treating them. That's stayed the same since she opened the clinic in Irbid.

She's still personally involved in almost every case that comes through the door, whether it's a routine checkup or something more serious, and she treats every animal with the same patience, cats included.
